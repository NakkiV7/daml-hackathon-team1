What is real, and what is not

Live on Canton DevNet

  • The mandate is a real contract on the ledger
  • Cap, period cap, allow-list, expiry and revocation are enforced by Mandate.daml
  • Every rejection is an AssertionFailed returned by Canton
  • Charge and revoke audit records are contracts on the ledger
  • Killing this backend loses nothing: the state lives on the ledger
  • Canton Coin really moves: a charge transfers Amulet and the payee's balance changes

Not done yet

  • Only 0.1 Canton Coin moves per settlement demo, and the agent holds about 5
  • The agent's spend cap is 500 but its coin balance is 5, so the cap is proven by refusal rather than by exhaustion
  • The allow-list is fixed when the mandate is granted
  • Period windows roll forward on the next charge, not on a timer
